Chaehyeon Hwang

I am a third-year undergraduate at Hankyong National University, majoring in Electrical & Electronic Engineering with a double major in Mathematics.

I am interested in how fine-tuning reshapes the predictive behavior of language models. Currently, I am studying why preference optimization (DPO) makes models more confident without making them more accurate, using controlled experiments and the geometry of the output distribution.

I also build things end to end. I designed and shipped whodict, a consent-based voice conversation app, on my own and filed a Korean patent application for it. Before that, I worked on genetic algorithms and Q-learning as an undergraduate researcher in the ML/DL Lab at my university.

Silver Prize, National University Students’ Mathematics Competition (Korean Mathematical Society).
