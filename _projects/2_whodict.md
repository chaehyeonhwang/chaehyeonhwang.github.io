**whodict** lets people talk with a voice they have permission to use — for example, a family member who has explicitly agreed to have their voice reproduced. Consent is part of the product, not an afterthought.

- Built end to end by myself: speech synthesis pipeline, app, and self-hosted GPU inference server.
- A Korean patent application covering the app's voice-verification method was filed in 2026 (pending).
- Currently preparing for release on Google Play and the App Store.
