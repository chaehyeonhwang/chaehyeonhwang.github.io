I'm a third-year undergraduate at Hankyong National University, majoring in Electrical & Electronic Engineering with a double major in Mathematics.

My research interest is in **understanding how fine-tuning reshapes the predictive behavior of language models** — in particular, how preference optimization (DPO / RLHF) changes a model's confidence and calibration, and how to describe those changes mathematically. I like questions where careful, controlled experiments and a clean mathematical picture can meet.

Beyond research, I build things end to end: I designed and shipped **whodict**, a consent-based voice conversation app, on my own and filed a Korean patent application for it.

Previously, I spent about six months as an undergraduate researcher in an ML/DL lab at my university, studying genetic algorithms and Q-learning on CartPole and Atari environments.

**Honors:** Silver Prize, National University Students' Mathematics Competition (Korean Mathematical Society) · Selected, Promising Startup Team 300 (data analysis) · Academic Excellence Scholarship
